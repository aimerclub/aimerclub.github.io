About AIMER

Where research
meets industry.

AIMER connects undergraduate and Master’s students with academic researchers and industry experts to work on real industrial challenges.

Canadian University Dubai and Etisalat UAE bring academic inquiry and industrial perspectives together through joint research and mentorship. Students can develop their ideas into working prototypes and potential publications, and explore pathways into postgraduate research.
