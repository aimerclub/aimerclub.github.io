01

The idea behind AIMER

Where research meets industry.

Good research starts with a question. Meaningful research connects it to the world.

AIMER brings undergraduate and Master’s students together with academic researchers and industry experts to explore industrial applications of artificial intelligence and autonomous systems.

Through collaborative research and mentorship, we aim to turn ideas into working prototypes and potential publications, while opening pathways toward postgraduate research.

A joint initiative of Canadian University Dubai and Etisalat UAE.

Academic partner

Canadian University Dubai

Academic research, student development, and a foundation for scientific inquiry.

Industry partner

Etisalat UAE

Industrial perspectives, real-world challenges, and opportunities for expert mentorship.

Our vision

“To be the UAE’s leading student community for industry-driven AI and machine autonomy research, from first project to first publication.”
